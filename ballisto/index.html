Is this for me?

Ballisto tells you how many clicks to move your sight when you shoot at a different distance from the one your rifle is zeroed at.

It also estimates the wind correction, either from where your last shot landed or from the crosswind speed. It's designed for 10 to 100 metre smallbore target shooting.

  • Works completely offline. It asks for no permissions at all, so it can't connect to the internet.
  • Free, with no adverts and no tracking.

What you need

  • An Android phone running Android 10 or later.
  • Your ammunition details: bullet weight and muzzle velocity, from the box or the maker's website.
  • Your sight's click value, for example 0.1 MRAD.

Install

  1. Tap the download button above on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will say it can't install apps from this source. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. You may see a Play Protect message about an unrecognised app. Tap More details, then Install anyway.

Using it

  1. Ammo: enter the name, bullet weight and muzzle velocity. Grey text shows a default or an example.
  2. Rifle: enter the rifle's name, click value and zero distance.
  3. Compute: enter the new distance to see how many clicks to move.
